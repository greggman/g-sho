Privacy

You can use g-sho without an account. Your searches, history and settings are then kept only in your browser. The dictionary is downloaded in small pieces as you search, and what you type isn't sent anywhere.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. You sign in with GitHub, and we keep:

  • your GitHub user ID (a number), username and display name, read from your public GitHub profile. We don't ask GitHub for your email address or for access to anything else, and we discard GitHub's access token right after reading your profile;
  • a record of each sign-in session (when it started and when it expires). Your browser holds the session in one cookie. We store only a hash of it.

While you're signed in, we also keep what this site syncs between your devices: your search history, the words you star or mark as known, your notes on words, and your display settings. Later, study decks will sync too. Your Anki connection settings stay on each device.

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. We don't sell or share your data.

Your data

In Settings → Account you can download everything stored for your account, or delete the account. Deleting it removes everything stored for it from our database right away. What's saved in your browser stays until you clear it.

Hosting

g-sho runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your requests and may keep logs of them, including IP addresses, for a short time to run and protect the service. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
